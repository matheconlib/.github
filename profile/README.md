# MathEconLib

A shared formal foundation in Lean 4 for research and applications in economics, co-founded by academic economists and [Axiom Math](https://axiommath.ai/) in 2026.

We build reusable definitions and results, with the goal of covering all mathematical areas of economics, including microeconomic theory, macroeconomic theory, and econometrics.
